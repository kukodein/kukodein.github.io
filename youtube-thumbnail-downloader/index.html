How to Download a YouTube Thumbnail

Downloading a YouTube thumbnail is simple: copy the video URL from your browser address bar or the YouTube app's share menu, paste it into the field above, and click Search. The tool will automatically detect the video ID and load every available thumbnail resolution.

YouTube stores thumbnails on its public image CDN (img.youtube.com) under predictable filenames — maxresdefault.jpg for Full HD, sddefault.jpg, hqdefault.jpg, and mqdefault.jpg for smaller sizes. Not all videos have a maxresdefault thumbnail; this tool checks availability and clearly marks which resolutions exist.

Downloaded thumbnails are standard JPEG images you can use for video promotion, content repurposing, presentations, or reference. Please respect copyright — the thumbnail image belongs to the video creator or channel.

Can I download thumbnails from Shorts?

Yes. Paste any youtube.com/shorts/ link and the tool will extract the thumbnail the same way it does for regular videos.

Why is Max Resolution (1280×720) sometimes unavailable?

YouTube only generates a maxresdefault thumbnail for videos uploaded in HD quality. Older or lower-resolution uploads may only have SD thumbnails available.
